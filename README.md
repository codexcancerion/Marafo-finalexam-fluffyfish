# Fluffy Shark – Flappy Bird-Inspired PWA Game

**Live Demo**: [Play Now](https://codexcancerion.github.io/Marafo-finalexam-fluffyfish/)

**Fluffy Shark** is a simple, addictive, and fun Flappy Bird-style game made as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Tap, flap, and swim your way through obstacles while racking up the highest score possible — all inside your browser!

## 🕹️ How to Play

* **Click or Tap to Flap** – Each tap gives the fish a small lift.
* **Avoid Pipes** – Don't touch the top or bottom of the obstacles.
* **Get Points** – Every obstacle passed scores one point.
* **Challenge Yourself** – Try to beat your high score!

---

## ⚙️ Technologies Used

* **HTML5 + CSS3 + JavaScript**
* **Canvas API** for rendering game objects
* **PWA Standards**: `manifest.json` + optional `serviceWorker.js`
* **Web Audio API** for effects and music

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Mechanics

* **Gravity System**: The fish is constantly pulled down — flap to stay afloat.
* **Collision Detection**: Game ends when hitting pipes or ground.
* **Score Tracking**: Updates in real-time as you pass obstacles.
* **Sound Effects**: Flap, crash, and background audio enhance immersion.
